Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Urban Hunt collects, uses, and protects information when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

Urban Hunt collects information in several ways as you interact with our platform. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and mobile number as essential account details. Depending on your role, you may also provide additional profile information such as your professional title, bio, company affiliation, or profile photograph. If you choose to add these details, they help other users understand your background and verify your credibility on the platform.

When you search for properties, browse listings, and use marketplace features, we record technical information about your activity. This includes which properties you view, how long you spend reviewing them, which search filters and preferences you use, and how you navigate through our platform. This activity data helps us understand which properties and features are most useful to our users and allows us to improve the relevance of search results and recommendations.

If you choose to save properties to your shortlist, we maintain a record of which properties you have saved and when you saved them. This information is stored to enable the saved properties feature and to help you return to listings you found interesting. Your saved properties list is private and visible only to you and to Urban Hunt systems that support the feature.

When you create a property listing, we collect detailed information about the property including its title, complete description, location address, municipality, property type, price, amenities, number of rooms and bathrooms, lot size, and other relevant details you provide. We also store any photographs, floor plans, property documents, or supplementary materials you upload. This information is essential for displaying your listing to potential buyers or renters and for allowing others to evaluate the property.

When you communicate with other users through property inquiries or our messaging system, we collect and store your messages, the timestamp of your communications, and related metadata. This includes inquiries you send to property sellers, responses you receive, and any ongoing conversations through our chat functionality. We retain this communication history to provide you with a complete conversation record, to help resolve disputes if needed, and to prevent misuse of our platform.

Urban Hunt collects technical and device information automatically when you use our platform. This includes your browser type and version, operating system, device type (mobile, tablet, desktop), approximate geographic location based on your IP address, referring website or application, and pages or features you access. We also record session information such as login times, logout times, and the duration of your visits. This technical data helps us maintain platform security, diagnose technical issues, understand how users interact with our services, and optimize performance.

How We Use Information

Urban Hunt uses the information we collect for several fundamental purposes that keep the platform operational and secure. We use your account information to create and maintain your profile, authenticate your identity, process your login requests, and provide you with personalized account settings and preferences. Your contact details enable us to send you important service notifications such as updates about your inquiries, responses from property owners, and alerts related to your account.

Property discovery and listing functionality depend on the information you provide and the information sellers submit. We use your search history and preferences to help you find properties that match your interests more effectively. When you save properties, these saved listings are stored and made available to you so you can return to interesting listings without having to search again. The detailed property information that sellers provide is processed and displayed so potential buyers can evaluate listings and determine whether a property meets their needs.

For sellers, we use the property information you submit to create and maintain your listings, help verify property details when appropriate, review listings for policy compliance before they appear in search results, and enable other users to discover your properties. We also use this information to help prevent duplicate or fraudulent listings and to detect policy violations.

Urban Hunt enables buyers and sellers to communicate through property inquiries and direct messaging. When you send an inquiry about a property, the seller receives your message and can respond directly. When you engage in conversations through our chat system, both parties can see the full conversation history. We process this communication data to deliver messages reliably, maintain conversation history, support customer service requests, investigate disputes, and prevent abuse of the messaging system. Communication between users may also be reviewed if we receive reports of misconduct or if we believe a violation of our terms or applicable law has occurred.

We use technical information, session data, and usage analytics to maintain platform security and prevent fraud. This helps us identify suspicious activity such as attempts to access accounts that do not belong to you, repeated failed login attempts, unusual patterns of messaging activity, or attempts to scrape property information in violation of our policies. We analyze this data to detect security threats, prevent unauthorized access, and protect both individual user accounts and the overall integrity of the platform.

Urban Hunt also uses information for customer support purposes. If you contact us with a question, report a problem, or need assistance, we use the information you provide along with your account history to understand your issue and provide an appropriate response. This may include reviewing your account activity, your messaging history, or your listings to better assist you.

Information is used to improve Urban Hunt services and features. By analyzing aggregated usage data, we understand which features are most valuable to users, where the platform may have technical issues, and how we can enhance the user experience. We never identify you personally in this analysis unless we are responding to a specific support request or investigating alleged misconduct.
