Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026. This policy explains how Urban Hunt collects, uses, and protects information when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

Urban Hunt collects information in several ways as you interact with our platform. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and mobile number as essential account details. Depending on your role, you may also provide additional profile information such as your professional title, bio, company affiliation, or profile photograph. If you choose to add these details, they help other users understand your background and verify your credibility on the platform.

When you search for properties, browse listings, and use marketplace features, we record technical information about your activity. This includes which properties you view, how long you spend reviewing them, which search filters and preferences you use, and how you navigate through our platform. This activity data helps us understand which properties and features are most useful to our users and allows us to improve the relevance of search results and recommendations.

If you choose to save properties to your shortlist, we maintain a record of which properties you have saved and when you saved them. This information is stored to enable the saved properties feature and to help you return to listings you found interesting. Your saved properties list is private and visible only to you and to Urban Hunt systems that support the feature.

When you create a property listing, we collect detailed information about the property including its title, complete description, location address, municipality, property type, price, amenities, number of rooms and bathrooms, lot size, and other relevant details you provide. We also store any photographs, floor plans, property documents, or supplementary materials you upload. This information is essential for displaying your listing to potential buyers or renters and for allowing others to evaluate the property.

When you communicate with other users through property inquiries or our messaging system, we collect and store your messages, the timestamp of your communications, and related metadata. This includes inquiries you send to property sellers, responses you receive, and any ongoing conversations through our chat functionality. We retain this communication history to provide you with a complete conversation record, to help resolve disputes if needed, and to prevent misuse of our platform.

Urban Hunt collects technical and device information automatically when you use our platform. This includes your browser type and version, operating system, device type (mobile, tablet, desktop), approximate geographic location based on your IP address, referring website or application, and pages or features you access. We also record session information such as login times, logout times, and the duration of your visits. This technical data helps us maintain platform security, diagnose technical issues, understand how users interact with our services, and optimize performance.

We collect information about how you use different areas of Urban Hunt, including how many times you search, which property types you browse most frequently, how often you use the messaging features, and whether you complete transactions or inquiries. This usage data is aggregated and analyzed to identify trends, measure platform adoption, and prioritize feature development based on user behavior.

How We Use Information

Urban Hunt uses the information we collect for several fundamental purposes that keep the platform operational and secure. We use your account information to create and maintain your profile, authenticate your identity, process your login requests, and provide you with personalized account settings and preferences. Your contact details enable us to send you important service notifications such as updates about your inquiries, responses from property owners, and alerts related to your account.

Property discovery and listing functionality depend on the information you provide and the information sellers submit. We use your search history and preferences to help you find properties that match your interests more effectively. When you save properties, these saved listings are stored and made available to you so you can return to interesting listings without having to search again. The detailed property information that sellers provide is processed and displayed so potential buyers can evaluate listings and determine whether a property meets their needs.

For sellers, we use the property information you submit to create and maintain your listings, help verify property details when appropriate, review listings for policy compliance before they appear in search results, and enable other users to discover your properties. We also use this information to help prevent duplicate or fraudulent listings and to detect policy violations.

Urban Hunt enables buyers and sellers to communicate through property inquiries and direct messaging. When you send an inquiry about a property, the seller receives your message and can respond directly. When you engage in conversations through our chat system, both parties can see the full conversation history. We process this communication data to deliver messages reliably, maintain conversation history, support customer service requests, investigate disputes, and prevent abuse of the messaging system. Communication between users may also be reviewed if we receive reports of misconduct or if we believe a violation of our terms or applicable law has occurred.

We use technical information, session data, and usage analytics to maintain platform security and prevent fraud. This helps us identify suspicious activity such as attempts to access accounts that do not belong to you, repeated failed login attempts, unusual patterns of messaging activity, or attempts to scrape property information in violation of our policies. We analyze this data to detect security threats, prevent unauthorized access, and protect both individual user accounts and the overall integrity of the platform.

Urban Hunt also uses information for customer support purposes. If you contact us with a question, report a problem, or need assistance, we use the information you provide along with your account history to understand your issue and provide an appropriate response. This may include reviewing your account activity, your messaging history, or your listings to better assist you.

Information is used to improve Urban Hunt services and features. By analyzing aggregated usage data, we understand which features are most valuable to users, where the platform may have technical issues, and how we can enhance the user experience. We never identify you personally in this analysis unless we are responding to a specific support request or investigating alleged misconduct.

Finally, we retain and use information as required by applicable law, to enforce our terms of service and other agreements, to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, to respond to lawful government requests when appropriate, and to protect the rights, property, and safety of Urban Hunt, our users, and the public.

Property and Listing Information

When you create a property listing, you are responsible for ensuring that all information you provide is accurate, current, and lawful. This includes property details, pricing, availability, location information, descriptions, photographs, amenities, and any other content you submit. You represent that you have the legal authority to list the property and to provide the information you have submitted. If you list a property on behalf of another person or organization, you confirm that you have appropriate authorization to do so.

Property listings are intended to be publicly visible to all users who access the Urban Hunt platform. The information in your listing, including the property title, description, photographs, location, price, and other details, may be displayed to prospective buyers, renters, or other users of the platform. By submitting a listing, you agree that this information will be accessible and searchable by the public. We may also share certain aggregated or anonymized information about listings in industry reports or analytics.

Urban Hunt reviews and moderates property listings to maintain the quality and integrity of the platform. We may approve, conditionally approve, reject, restrict, temporarily hide, or remove listings that we believe are incomplete, misleading, fraudulent, discriminatory, unlawful, duplicative of other listings, or inconsistent with our policies and guidelines. We may modify listing information if we identify obvious errors such as incorrect contact information, incomplete required fields, or formatting problems. If your listing is removed or restricted, we will attempt to provide you with an explanation. In some cases, we may suspend your ability to create new listings temporarily while we investigate concerns.

It is important to understand that Urban Hunt's review or verification of a listing does not constitute a legal guarantee of property ownership, a valuation of the property, a professional property inspection, a title search, a certificate of occupancy, or any form of transaction guarantee. Urban Hunt provides tools to connect buyers and sellers but does not participate in the actual transaction and does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, safety, or suitability of any property information. Users are responsible for conducting independent verification of all property details before making any financial or legal commitments.

Sellers remain responsible for updating property listings when information changes. If a property is no longer for sale or rent, the price changes, availability changes, or any details are no longer accurate, sellers should update or remove the listing. Sellers should also remove or correct listings if property conditions change, if the property has been sold or rented, or if they no longer have authorization to advertise the property. Urban Hunt is not responsible for outdated or inaccurate information provided by sellers.

Photographs and visual materials you upload as part of a listing are subject to Urban Hunt's content policies. Photographs must depict the actual property and must not contain misleading alterations, fabricated images, or materials that are inappropriate. If we identify violations in visual content, we may remove specific photographs or the entire listing. You grant Urban Hunt a license to host, display, store, and use uploaded property images as necessary to provide the platform and to improve Urban Hunt services.

Communications and Messaging

Urban Hunt enables direct communication between users through property inquiries and a messaging system. When you send an inquiry about a property, your message is delivered to the seller and stored in both your account and the seller's account so both parties can reference the conversation. Similarly, when you engage in multi-message conversations through our chat system, both participants can access the full conversation history. This functionality allows buyers and sellers to negotiate, ask questions, and coordinate property viewings or transactions.

We store communication data including the content of your messages, the date and time messages were sent, and the participants involved in conversations. This data is retained so that you can access your message history, so disputes can be investigated if needed, and so we can prevent abuse of messaging features. Communication data is not visible to users other than the participants in the conversation unless we are required to disclose it in response to a legal request or are investigating potential violations of our policies.

Urban Hunt may review or access communication data to investigate complaints, respond to legal requests, enforce our terms of service, prevent fraud or abuse of the platform, protect the safety and security of our users, and comply with applicable law. For example, if we receive a report that a user is using our messaging system to harass, threaten, or engage in fraud, we may review the relevant messages to investigate. If we identify clear violations such as threats, sexual harassment, fraud schemes, or spam, we may warn the user, restrict their messaging privileges, or suspend their account.

Urban Hunt may send you notifications related to your inquiries, messages, account activity, and platform updates. These notifications may be delivered through email, in-platform alerts, or mobile push notifications if you have a mobile application installed. You may adjust your notification preferences through your account settings to reduce the frequency or type of notifications you receive, though we reserve the right to send critical security alerts or account notifications even if you have disabled other notifications.

Messages and inquiries are provided on an as-available basis. While we work to ensure reliable message delivery, Urban Hunt does not guarantee that every message will be delivered successfully, that messages will be received immediately, or that the system will always be available. Communication delays or service interruptions may occur due to technical issues, platform maintenance, or other circumstances beyond our control. Users should not rely on Urban Hunt messaging as the sole method of communication for time-critical matters and may consider supplementing important conversations with direct contact information such as phone numbers or email addresses.

Cookies, Sessions, and Technical Information

Urban Hunt uses cookies, web beacons, session identifiers, and similar technologies to recognize returning users, remember your login state, maintain the security of your session, and track how you use the platform. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that allow Urban Hunt to recognize you on subsequent visits. Session identifiers are unique codes assigned to your current browsing session and are used to maintain your authenticated login state while you are actively using the platform.

Some cookies are essential for platform functionality such as maintaining your login session and enabling basic features. Other cookies support analytics, preferences, and improvements to your experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences, but disabling cookies may limit your ability to use certain Urban Hunt features. Some browsers offer Do Not Track settings; however, Urban Hunt does not currently recognize or respond to Do Not Track signals.

We use analytics services to understand how users interact with Urban Hunt, which features are used most frequently, where technical problems may occur, and how the platform performs across different devices and browsers. Analytics data is collected through cookies, pixels, and similar tracking technologies. This data is typically aggregated and does not personally identify individual users, though in some cases analytics tools may use information from your account to track your behavior across multiple visits.
